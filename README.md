# LEGO Jokers

A small content mod adding 15 Jokers and 1 deck, themed around LEGO and its community.

# Content

<details><summary>Jokers (click if you don't want to discover them in play)</summary>

Classic Joker: Face cards held in hand at end of round become Diamonds.

Greebled Joker: Each scored 2 or 3 adds a retrigger to all other played cards.

AFOL: X2 Mult if played hand has a scoring face card and a scoring non-face card.

Licensed Joker: Earn $1 when a face card is drawn to hand. Lose $1 when a face card is played or discarded.

Nice Part Usage: When scored, each card gives +1 Mult per round since its rank was last scored.

Sticker Sheet: Sell this card to create a Rare Joker with a random Sticker (Must have room)

Ultimate Collector's Joker: This Joker gains +18 Chips each time you obtain a Rare or Uncommon Joker.

My Own Creation: The next valid scoring hand becomes a new poker hand with its own Planet card.

Illegal Technique: 1 in 7 cards are temporarily debuffed each round. Debuffed cards in first hand of round gain a random Enhancement.

Convention Badge: This Joker gains X0.5  Mult when an unplayed poker hand is played. Only gives Mult when the last such hand is played.

Frog: X4 Mult. Must play a 4.

SNOT Brick: All 9s are considered 6s. All 5s are considered 2s.

Big Ugly Rock Piece: Adds one Stone Card into drawn cards. At end of round, destroy all Stone Cards in deck.

Cheese Slope: Earn $12 at end of round, reduces by $3 for each other Joker you have.

Brick Separator: If first hand of round has only 1 card, split it into 2 cards with half its rank.

</details>

Rainbow Deck: Start run with 13 additional Wild Cards in deck
